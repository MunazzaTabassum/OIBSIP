# 🛍️ Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship**.

The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify customer purchasing behaviour, sales trends, product performance, and business insights using Python.

---

# 🎯 Objective

- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics.
- Identify best-performing product categories.
- Analyze shopping mall performance.
- Generate business recommendations based on data.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

### Sales Trend

- Monthly sales remained relatively stable throughout the analysis period.
- The decline in the final month is due to incomplete transaction data.

### Customer Demographics

- Female customers contributed more transactions than male customers.
- Customers belong to a wide range of age groups.

### Product Performance

- Clothing generated the highest revenue.
- Shoes and Technology also contributed significantly.
- Books, Food & Beverage, and Souvenir categories generated comparatively lower revenue.

### Correlation Analysis

- Quantity and Price show a positive relationship.
- Customer Age has very little impact on purchasing behaviour.

### Shopping Mall Performance

- Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon generated the highest revenue.
- Some malls have opportunities for improvement through targeted marketing.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory planning for high-performing categories such as Clothing, Shoes, and Technology.
- Develop promotional campaigns for lower-performing categories.
- Analyze strategies used by top-performing shopping malls and replicate successful practices.
- Launch location-specific marketing campaigns for lower-performing malls.
- Continue monitoring sales trends for better inventory and staffing decisions.
